Our Story

Cina Designs & Events began as a shared passion between a mother and daughter who love creating meaningful, handmade pieces for the people they care about. What started as a small hobby quickly grew into a small business dedicated to turning heartfelt ideas into beautiful designs.

Each piece is thoughtfully designed and carefully crafted with attention to detail, quality materials, and lots of love. We take pride in creating items that become part of your home, celebrations, and memories.
